# LilBit BigLife

A private, mobile-first reflection companion **for LillyClaire**.

LilBit BigLife helps her check the fear of disappointing a close friend against what that friend has actually told her. The assumption is always for the good.

**Live:** https://cb512175.github.io/lilbit-biglife/

## Privacy

Everything is saved only on this phone (in the browser's local storage). There are no accounts, uploads, or analytics. Nothing is shared unless she shares it.

## What it does

- **Friends:** Keep up to 5 close friends, and up to 5 things that disappoint or hurt each one most. She can ask a friend (with a copyable / shareable prompt) and mark answers *In her own words*, or write *What she told me*.
- **I'm Worried:** A gentle check-in. If she did something on the list, the app offers a kind way to make it right. If she didn't, it helps her trust her friend and herself.
- **My Trends:** A picture of how often a worry was only a worry — totals, per-friend counts, a simple weekly/monthly chart, history she can delete, and a way to mark a real hurt as *made it right*.

Example friends (Maya, Jordan, Priya) are clearly fictional and can be removed. Real friends' names and notes stay on the device.

## Not therapy

This is a reflection tool, not therapy, and not a clinical service. If worry feels too big, talk to a parent or a trusted adult. In the US, call or text 988 if you ever feel unsafe or hopeless.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8765
```

Then visit http://localhost:8765
